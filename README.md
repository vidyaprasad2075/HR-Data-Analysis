# HR Analytics: Employee Performance & Attrition Analysis (Power BI)

## 📌 Executive Project Overview
This business intelligence project delivers a comprehensive workforce analytics solution designed to monitor corporate headcount dynamics, track historical hiring trends, and diagnose the underlying drivers of employee attrition. Processing data for a baseline workforce of 2,500 corporate employees across 5 UAE Emirates, this interactive Power BI dashboard provides HR leadership with deep visibility into organizational health, attendance benchmarks, and departmental retention challenges.

By transforming raw HR transactional records into clear, actionable visual insights, this tool enables data-driven succession planning and proactive talent management strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Technical Implementation
* **Power BI Desktop:** Core platform utilized for data mashups, dashboard UI/UX styling, and interactive component delivery.
* **Power Query (ETL):** Employed to ingest, clean, and profile unstructured HR files, enforcing strict data types and handling missing evaluations.
* **DAX Modeling:** Engineered calculated columns and measures to dynamically track moving totals, attrition percentages, and weighted evaluation scores.

---

## 📈 Key Human Resources Benchmarks (KPIs)
Through targeted data aggregation, organization-wide baseline talent metrics were established:
* **Total Headcount Managed:** 2,500 employees.
* **Active Workforce:** 1,899 employees.
* **Resigned Attrition Count:** 480 employees.
* **Calculated Corporate Attrition Rate:** 19.20%.
* **Average Attendance Compliance Score:** 87.55%.
* **Average Employee Performance Rating:** 76.48%.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Business Insights & Workforce Diagnostics

### 1. Retention & Attrition Deep-Dive
The organization exhibits a structural attrition rate of **19.20%** ($480 \text{ total resignations}$). By evaluating this attrition against demographic variables, regional performance levels, and length of service, HR teams can actively flag active departments displaying abnormal turnover signals before they hit critical operational thresholds.

### 2. Performance vs. Attendance Optimization
The dashboard maps a strong visual baseline comparing an **87.55% Average Attendance Score** against a **76.48% Average Performance Score**. Segmenting these dual metrics across the 7 corporate departments allows leadership to isolate whether operational drops are driven by scheduling compliance conflicts or fundamental training gaps.

### 3. Regional & Historical Hiring Demographics
Tracking historical recruitment distributions from 2021 through 2024 across 5 distinct UAE Emirates reveals clear geographic talent clusters. This regional distribution modeling guides corporate recruiters on where to focus local talent acquisition drives and optimize regional office resource allocation.
